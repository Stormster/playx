Live · crowd played

PlayX

One game, everyone watching at the controls. Vote on the next button. In Democracy the top vote wins each round; in Anarchy every press goes in.

Controls

Tap the buttons, or click or Tab to the game screen to use the keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, Z or J for A, X or K for B, Enter for Start, tap Shift for Select, Q and E for L and R. Hold a key to repeat. Anarchy repeats moves for up to 3 seconds, then pauses for 2 seconds; release and press again. Democracy renews your single vote until release. Shift votes on release. Pick ×2, ×3 or ×5 (or press 1, 2, 3, 5) to vote for several steps at once.

House rules

Releasing Pokemon gets undone automatically. Start and Select need twice the votes of the runner-up. Play time only counts while someone is watching.
